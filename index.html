YC Founder Outreach

Pick a YC batch, find founder emails, and write personalised cold emails from one template. Free without any keys; everything you type stays in your browser.

2. Email template

Click to insert: {first_name}{company}{one_liner}{batch}{my_name}{portfolio}{github}{resume}  

Lines like resume: {resume} are dropped when you leave that field empty. Email labels: verified / unverified come from Apify (step 4), on site is listed on the company's website, guess is the most common pattern (right about 80% of the time) and may bounce.

3. Pick a YC batch

4. Find verified emails (optional)

Without this you get emails listed on company sites plus pattern guesses like first@domain (right about 80% of the time). For verified emails, paste your own Apify API token. Lookups run from your browser straight to Apify on your account; the token is never sent to this site's server and isn't saved.

Open source. Company and founder data comes from YC's public directory. Please write to people one at a time and respect "no".
